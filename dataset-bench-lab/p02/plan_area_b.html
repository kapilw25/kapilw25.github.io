Plan B: a humanoid that walks and carries in a warehouse, tested where every tabletop benchmark stops

  • 🎥 The gap. Every 2024 to 2026 generalization test is a tabletop Franka; nobody ships a test plus a training set for a humanoid that walks and manipulates in an industrial scene.
  • 📉 The stakes. Policies at 97 to 98 percent on LIBERO fall to 0.0 on LIBERO-PRO and to 28 percent on RoboLab; the standard suites no longer separate methods.
  • 🏭 The move. One Isaac Lab-Arena generator puts a Unitree G1 in SimReady warehouses: pick, walk, place chains under 12 to 15 factors with held-out levels and compositions, typed twins, labelled failures, per-step progress, exposed as an RL environment with a frozen rubric judge as the secondary score.
  • 🎯 The bet, pre-registered. GR00T N1.x co-trained on each of six rival sets against OURS under one recipe; the home-game win counts only on held-out levels and compositions, backed by external Franka checks we do not own. A hypothesis until the score table has numbers.

Reading the table: tabletop Franka generalization is crowded, two entries already validate against real robots, and one ships 20k perturbed demos. Nobody ships a generalization test plus a training set for a humanoid that walks and manipulates in industrial scenes. Isaac Lab-Arena already provides the pieces: a Unitree G1 and a Franka, atomic skills (pick, walk, place) chained into long tasks, subtask predicates, sensitivity analysis, thousands of parallel environments, and policy clients for GR00T, pi-0.5 and DreamZero, but no leaderboard and no perturbation benchmark [S1]. NVIDIA's own Arena G1 sets hold 50 and 200 demos and the GR00T-X set holds 102 G1 loco-manipulation trajectories (notes_01.md (lab notes, private), T1 tier). That is the open slot: the same warehouse and smart-space story as Area A, on a humanoid.

🧾 Recipe and skeleton

R1 for every column: copy demos, then grade own rollouts with the success predicate [0/1 reward, as in SimpleVLA-RL [S10]]; same 10 seeds per task, same steps, same rollout budget. R2 track for OURS only: per-step progress as a dense reward, reported in its own row. Base policy: GR00T N1.x through the Arena client (owner decision), so Franka rival data co-trains with the G1 seeds through its multi-body action heads.

⛔ Risks and limits

🏷️ risk 📉 what it costs 🛡️ answer in the plan
home-game critique reviewers discount a win on our own test held-out levels and compositions never generated for training; audit probes; external checks; ablations that separate generator from novelty
no real robot REALM and RoboLab both validated against real runs owner decision for v0: sim only, stated as a limitation; audit probes, held-out levels and the Franka external checks carry the argument; a RoboArena partner is the v1 option
GR1 instead of G1 GR00T-X's 240k GR1 tabletop demos become a near home-game rival pick G1, where public data is 352 trajectories in total
humanoid contact fidelity walking-while-carrying is where simulators are weakest report per-skill results; keep a Franka variant for external checks
judge bias a VLM judge is fooled by viewpoint and contact; the best of 13 judges reaches 0.77 balanced accuracy on FailBench [S15] judge frozen after one validation on held-out human-labelled clips; agreement reported; predicates stay the headline, the judge is secondary
GPU cost Isaac Sim needs RTX-class GPUs; Arena evaluation is parallel but rendering is not free budget on the cluster; CPU-only stages first (spec, assets, protocol)
scope benchmark, dataset, harness, leaderboard, metadata build order 1 to 10 above; a v0 with 6 factors and 20 tasks is a paper

🗺️ First five steps, cheapest first

  1. Write the test spec: factors, levels, held-out rule, task list, episode counts, statistics; owner file literature/area_b_test_spec.md (to be created, no GPU).
  2. Stand up Isaac Lab-Arena with the SimReady warehouse and one G1 pick-walk-place chain; record 10 seed demos; script papers/p02-humanoid-warehouse/generate/arena_seed_demos.py (to be created, one RTX GPU).
  3. Mimic expansion plus twins for two factors (object colour, camera) with replay verification; script papers/p02-humanoid-warehouse/generate/twins.py (to be created).
  4. Evaluate GR00T N1.x zero-shot and after R1 on the v0 test; confirm the test is failed by current policies, otherwise change the factors before building more.
  5. Add rivals as co-training columns, then the loop from Plan 02 on a dev split of our own factors.
