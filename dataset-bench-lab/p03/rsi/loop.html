Proposal side

how search effort is spent

Test before walk

Every idea first gets a single-step test (16 to 36 fresh calls, about $1 to $5); a 30-room walk (about $25) runs only when the test predicts a fall.

Evidence-aware ledger

Every try is logged with its hypothesis, settings, scores, cause and next steps, so a dead idea is not tried again.

Explore untried parts

When rounds stop deepening the cliff, change a part never touched yet (the camera, the memory rules, the renderer).

One walk at a time

Walks run one after another on the Mac, so the plan's usage window is not spent twice as fast.

Selection side

which results count

Leak critic

The best no-notes guess must stay at 25% or below on the long questions; every run carries random and heuristic baselines.

Cliff gate

At least 80% at 1 to 2 rooms and at most 33% at the longest distances, own room at least 85%, on 3 fresh seeds.

Noise band

One wrong answer in 5 to 10 long questions moves accuracy 10 to 20 points; a fall must clear that band (10 points here).

Cost rule

At most $50 a walk; the search stops spending on an idea the test already killed.
