50 sub-failure modes as Isaac scenes

One Isaac Sim scene (.usda) per v12 sub-failure mode: 15 state changes, and the same v12 robot action asked after K = 1, 3, 5, 10 and 15 of them, so each mode gets its own cliff along K (version 2: 22 modes ask about the case of picture 1 with K - 1 look-alike cases in between; every picture has its own camera shift and light). Answers are actions in the robot's own frame (points within max(0.3 m, 10% of the distance), lists, commands); keys come from the scene's state and are re-read from the Isaac stage on the GPU. Built on the Mac; not yet compiled on the GPU (pictures and physics outcomes come from there).
