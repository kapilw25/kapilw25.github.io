P03 · Spatial Benchmark Survey

SpatialGym-Bench as an agent benchmark: an agent starts at a fixed camera view in a simulated warehouse (Isaac Sim 6.0, SimReady assets), may walk, turn and look up to 10 times on a grid, then answers. Every step it takes (tool outputs, depths, positions, what it believes after each move) is graded against exact simulator state at evaluation time, and twin scenes with one 3D change are scored as pairs.
