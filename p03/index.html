Spatial Benchmark Survey

39 spatial-reasoning benchmarks and methods side by side: what the model sees, how many questions and categories, where the answers come from, whether the agent moves, whether intermediate steps are graded, and whether paired (twin) items are scored together. The SpatialClaw columns are that paper's Table 3 scores on a shared Gemma 4-31B backbone.

How to filter. Click section chips to show or hide a section; type in the box under any header to filter that column (numeric columns also take >1000, <500 or 1000-3000); click a header to sort; the address bar keeps the view, so a filtered table can be shared as a link.

Legend. Moves: the model can move a camera or act before answering. Steps: intermediate steps are graded against ground truth. Twins: matched items are scored as a group. Threat: how close the benchmark comes to the two claims of SpatialGym-Bench, (a) every step graded against exact simulator state at evaluation time and (b) twin scenes with one 3D change scored as pairs. A dash in a score column means the method cannot take that input (VADAR: single images only).

Sources. Every row comes from a full read of the paper; every link was checked (HTTP 200) on 2026-09-30. Counts are the benchmark's test items as stated in its paper; a second number in brackets is the released or corrected set. Built 2026-09-30.
